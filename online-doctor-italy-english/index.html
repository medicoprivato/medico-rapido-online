Doctors in Italy: online written medical consultations

Information for visitors and residents in Italy who need a private written medical assessment in English, without phone calls or video appointments.

What can you request?

  • Non-emergency medical guidance
  • Assessment of medication renewal requests
  • Medical report interpretation
  • Women's health and gynecological advice
  • Menopause and natural fertility

A prescription or certificate is not guaranteed. An in-person examination may be required.

How does it work?

  1. Review the service conditions and confirm that the language and clinical situation can be safely accommodated.
  2. Pay the consultation fee and submit your medical request using the designated channel.
  3. Receive a written assessment, normally within 24 hours.

Private prescriptions and the Italian health service

A private prescription may be issued only when clinically appropriate and legally permitted. Medico Subito does not issue prescriptions covered by the Italian National Health Service (SSN). Contraceptive prescriptions and emergency contraception are not offered.
